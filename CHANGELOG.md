# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-10-31
- Projector mode: a shared screen (TV, projector) hosts the room without a seat and shows the whole table in big, with the join link and code, the current turn and the staged plays. Each player plays on their own device in a compact view: their hand, one line per opponent (turn, favors, protection, discards, what they know of the hand) and only the plays that involve them. A button switches to the full table and back.
- Network protocol 2 (spectator connections): players on 0.2.0 must reload.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-10-04
- Game speed setting (slow / normal / fast) in the game bar and settings: slows the staged plays and the bots, which now wait for the previous play to be shown.
- The table fits the screen: the log and chat scroll inside their panel instead of stretching the page.
- Card values are centered in their medallion (lining figures).

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-04
- Full 2019 Love Letter rules (21 cards, Spy and Chancellor, 2-6 players, tie and Spy bonus rules).
- Solo against bots (offline), online rooms with code, optional password, invite link.
- Peer-to-peer play with automatic host migration, reconnect to the same seat, autopilot for absent players.
- Autosave, resume, JSON export/import.
- Hand-drawn SVG card art, card flips, shared-layout card movement, staged plays, round and victory screens.
- Synthesized sound effects, volume, reduced-motion option.
- French and English, ~60 themes.
